The Auwald Theocracy/Sorcracy/kingdom(?) The region is a grassland broken by steppe, with massive lengths of flat, straight ground interrupted by occasional deep ravines and jagged crags that cut through the land. It is a place known for its abundance of magbeasts, monsters, and natural geographic defenses.
