### Magis Laboratorium
The Magis Laboratorium operates from two sites: its main branch in Valcris, the capital, and its secondary branch at Morgenor Keep. The Morgenor facility works closely alongside Ravania's Excissors, who focus on slaying demihumans, monsters, and other profane creatures.

Within the Magis Laboratorium exists a hierarchy of ranks beginning with

**Primus Magis** -  Commands one of the two branches (Valcris or Morgenor). Oversees all research, resources, and personnel at their location.

**Magia Circulus** -  Those bound to the Greschian Circle. Short, emphasizes the binding that defines them, they hold immense power over the Laboratories and are enlisted to make new expeidtions to ancient Greschia.

**Magicus Inquis** - Investigates magical accidents, rogue practitioners, or forbidden research within the Laboratorium itself. Ensures no one dabbles in prohibited magic. works closely with the Divinor Imperium.

**Grexplorator** - Field researcher who travels to Ancient Greschia to help bind new Greschian sorcerers, find artifacts, or investigate dangerous magical sites in and on the way to Greschia. Often works with Excissors.

**Sigilmeister** - Specialist rank for the empire's best Sigilists. Create the most complex enchantments for military and imperial use.

**Artifex** - Fully trained practitioner who conducts independent research or creates enchantments/alchemical compounds. 

**Adeptus** - . Advanced student who has proven basic competency and chosen a specialization Greschian sorcery, Sigilism, Alchemy, Morphalhemy, etc.

**Discipulus** - Student. Entry level, learning basic magical theory, alchemy, or sigilism. Ages typically nine after being checked for potency.
