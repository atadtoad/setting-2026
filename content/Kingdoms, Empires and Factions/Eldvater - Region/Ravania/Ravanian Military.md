The Ravanian military highly values mages and sorcerers, especially with the rising popularity of Sigilism. Yet these magic users remain rare. The army's main strength consists of cavalry wielding riding sabers and infantry clad in mail, scale armor, and dark gambesons. Infantry carry dueling sabers and crescent spears, while the royal guard wields heavy longaxe halberds.

#### Military Ranks

**Primus Praetorius** - Commander of the Praetorian guard.
**Praetorian** - Memeber of the Royal guard, 

**Primus Praefectus** - Commander of multiple Legions, General of the armies, presiding over multiple Legatus and 

**Legatus** - Legion commander, supreme field commander. Commands 4,000-6,000 men. Always Equestrian or Proconsul rank.

**Zenturio** - Commands 80-100 infantry.  Veterans of multiple campaigns usually of Equestrian rank.

**Ligonier** - Professional soldier, career military. usually of lower Equestrian rank. 
**Decurio** Squad leader of 10 Horseriders. First promotion for promising Horsemen, usually still Plebian class. slightly above Decarch.
**Decarch** - Squad leader of 10 men. First promotion for promising soldiers, usually still Plebian class. 

**Recruit** - New recruit undergoing mandatory service, lowest rank.


#### Magical Ranks within the Army
**Divinor Arma** - Individuals priestesses carrying the image of the Kaiser and the Ravanian flag into battle, its rank is outside of the heirchey as nor Legatus or Greschian Sorcerers disobey their orders. they follow only the order of the ranks in the church. yet still has ability to command those in the army to an extent. this rank can be given to any within the church for temporary time.

**Greschian Sorcerers** - Dangerous, highly powerful sorcrers, they do not bow before any rank lower than a **Primus Praefectus** or the **Praetorians** and the **Divinor Arma**, Equivelent to Zenturio in Rank officialy, however within their own, they measure their influence by strength. and never simply follow the orders of Legatus, nor are they punished by Legatus. in turn however, they are punished by the **Primus Praefectus** themselves.

**Magister Sigilorum** - Master of Sigilsists, manages battlefield enchantments and fortification magic.  equivelent in rank to Zenturios

**Sigilorum** - attached to companies, responsible for enchanting weapons, armor, and fortifications. they are equivelent in rank to Ligonier
