# Recruitment and Training of Intelligence agents

The intelligence agents of Ravania, the Insitus, Delators, Notarius, Silentium, and Mortis, come from multiple origins.

Some are children who lost their families to wars, plagues, or campaigns. No one to miss them. Raised in special imperial orphanages from a young age, they are trained, given a home, and made to see the agency as their family.

Others come from within the armies of Ravania. Soldiers who show exceptional language skills, acting ability, or adaptability make prime candidates for the agency. So too do those who are capable fighters but have suffered trauma that leaves them malleable. The empire finds them and offers purpose, a new direction, a reason to keep going.

Those enlisted young enough go through rigorous testing first. Potential recruits are placed on special lists and watched for years. Their officers or outside instructors assess how convincingly they lie, how strongly they form attachments, how quickly they learn new languages, how well they memorize details, how adaptable they are, and how they hold up under hard pressure. Most wash out, becoming regular soldiers, clerks, or servants. They never know they were being tested for something else.

Those who pass are told they have been selected. They are removed from their previous lives and brought into the fold. For recruits drawn from the general population, their families are quietly informed their son or daughter died in an accident. A small funeral is held. The old name is buried with it.

From there, recruits are moved to secret training facilities in isolated regions or remote keeps. The training agencies are divided by specialization, those dedicated to Mortis, Silentium, or Insitus, though a handful of facilities share the same core curriculum for initial Candidatus and Notarius training, usually spread across different regions of Ravania. Here the first lesson is simple: the empire is all that matters. It is their family. The other recruits are their family.

The training itself is brutal. Recruits are forced to speak only in target languages, those of Lorden, Aldergard, Mordantis, and others, for months at a time. They are isolated in cells for weeks, then suddenly surrounded by warmth and community, then stripped of it again. They are taught to distrust everyone, including fellow trainees. Those who make mistakes are beaten by their peers. Instructors quietly arrange mock friendships between recruits, only to later force them to betray those same companions as part of exercises. Trust is treated as a weapon to be studied, not a comfort to be kept.

Combat training is included but kept limited. Their time is too valuable for soldiering. Instead, the emphasis falls on high-intensity endurance work, running, climbing, vaulting, and acrobatics. Most trainees are taught to flee before they are taught to fight.

The intellectual curriculum is equally demanding. Recruits study encryption, dead drops, coded messages, the histories and court structures of target kingdoms, and advanced memorization techniques. The specifics vary between agencies, each of which competes fiercely to produce the finest Insitus, Silentium and Mortis.

Graduates who return to the capital, Valcris, have their housing, food, and medical care covered by the empire. When on active duty, they are on their own.

Many Ravanians speak of these agents in hushed reverence, legends from the shadows. The agents themselves serve for different reasons. Some are driven by genuine honor and loyalty to Ravania. Some are too psychologically broken to want a normal life. Others believe that completing their mission will finally earn them something they have never had: a place to belong, a purpose, or perhaps even redemption.

The Insitus, Silentium, and Mortis represent three distinct paths within these agencies. Each focuses on different methods and objectives, and each carries its own internal hierarchy of rank.
# Intelligence Command

**Praefectus Officiorum** - Supreme Spymaster and Master of Offices. Controls all imperial intelligence networks and reports directly to the Kaiser. This position is always held by someone of Equestrian or Senatore rank.

**Magister Officiorum** - Directly below the Praefectus Officiorum, the Magister Officiorum oversees the operational networks of all three agent paths, Insitus, Mortis, and Silentium, across foreign kingdoms. They coordinate between the regional Magisters, manage the flow of intelligence upward to the Praefectus, and ensure the three paths do not work at cross purposes when operating in the same region.

**Magister Insitorum** - Runs the full network of Insiti agents across a specific kingdom or region. Always operates from within Ravania or a secure location and is never directly exposed in the field. Responsible for managing dead drops, coordinating intelligence gathering, and deciding which Insitus agents to activate and when. Reports directly to the Magister Officiorum. A Magister Insitorum is typically assigned to one kingdom or region for years at a time, building intimate knowledge of its political landscape and the agents embedded within it.


##### Agent Ranks

The Mortis, Insitus, Notarius, Delator and Silentium paths are equal in standing, and advancement within each follows the same structure across the Intelligence Command.

**Primus Mortis** - A senior operative with multiple successful high-value eliminations to their name. Trusted with the most dangerous assignments and capable of leading small teams of Mortis agents. Often enhanced with Sigilist protections or Aurian blessings for combat advantage. Reaching this rank typically requires the ability to cast Greschian Sorceries, a necessity given the difficulty and danger of the targets they are sent to eliminate. However, because Greschian Sorceries are highly identifiable to those who know what to look for, a Primus Mortis deployed outside Ravanian borders operates under strict expectation: if captured, they are to take their own life before interrogation. Escape is preferred. Capture without death is not acceptable.

**Mortis** - Elite spy-assassins tasked with high-value eliminations and dangerous reconnaissance. Unlike the Silentium, the Mortis prioritize completing the kill above all else; collateral and political fallout are secondary concerns. Junior Mortis frequently draw on assigned Greschian Sorcerers to assist with scrying and locating their targets. When operating outside Ravanian borders however, this collaboration is kept to a minimum and handled with extreme caution, a Greschian Sorcerer operating openly in foreign territory is as damning a piece of evidence as the body left behind. In such cases, sorcerous support is either avoided entirely or conducted at a distance from within Ravanian borders where possible. It is in part this limitation that drives Mortis agents to eventually master Greschian Sorceries themselves, internalizing the capability rather than relying on an outside party that creates additional exposure.

**Primus Silentium** - An assassin who has made dozens of kills without ever drawing suspicion. Trusted with the most sensitive eliminations: foreign royalty, allied nobles who have become liabilities, or internal traitors at the highest levels. Capable of arranging deaths months in advance through elaborate and untraceable setups.

**Silentium** - Assassination specialists whose defining skill is ensuring targets die quietly, leaving no political complications, no riots, and no trail leading back to the empire.

**Primus Insitus** - A senior deep cover agent who has successfully completed at least one major mission. Trusted to manage multiple assets and recruit new local informants. May coordinate several junior Insitus operating in the same region, often without ever making direct contact with them.

**Insitus** - Deep cover agents embedded for years within foreign courts or enemy organizations. The most dangerous and isolating role in the Intelligence Command. They receive their missions through coded missives and instructions passed down from Magister Insitorum.

**Sanctus Delator** - Where a standard Delator roots out heresy through information and denunciation, the Sanctus Delator acts on it directly. Primarily deployed against internal threats, targeting heretical leaders and organized dissent within the empire itself. They are known for killings that are ruthless, theatrical, and deliberately symbolic, designed not just to eliminate a target but to send a message to those who might follow them.

**Delator** - An alternative path within the Intelligence Command, most commonly taken by agents who are deeply religious rather than those suited to the long patience of Insitus work or the precision of Silentium. Often held in parallel with a position in the church. Delators are professional informants and denouncers, tasked with identifying and reporting sedition, heresy, and disloyalty to the empire. They are widely hated but considered necessary. Most wear masks to protect their identities. Their religious zeal makes them effective, but also unpredictable. Other agents within the Command are wise to be careful around them, a Delator who suspects heresy does not distinguish easily between a foreign enemy and a colleague whose methods they find troubling.

**Notarius** - Intelligence analyst and record keeper. Not a field role. Manages incoming reports, decodes messages, and identifies patterns across gathered intelligence. The unseen foundation that keeps the Command functioning.

**Candidatus** - An agent who has completed training but has yet to prove themselves in the field. Their first mission determines whether they earn full standing as a Mortis, Insitus, or Silentium. If the mission is completed but falls short of its full objectives, they may be assigned another before a final judgment is made. Those who fail outright are executed, both for what they know and for what they have proven themselves incapable of.
