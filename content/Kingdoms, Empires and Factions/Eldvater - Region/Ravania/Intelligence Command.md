# Recruitment and Training of Intelligence agents

The Intellegience agents of Ravania, the Insitus, Delators, Silentium and so come from multiple origins, some are children who lost families in wars, plagues, or campaigns. No one to miss them. Raised in special imperial orphanages from a young age, trained, given a home, and made the agency their home.

Some within the armies of Ravania who are individuals who show exceptional language skills, acting ability, or adaptability which makes them prime candidtes for the agency, or pehraps soldiers that are capable  but have suffered trauma that makes them malleable. The empire finds them and offers purpose and a new goal in their life. 

Those enlisted young enough go through rigrous testing where potential recruits are placed in special lists where they're watched for years. and their officers or outside instructors asses them for how well and convicngly they lie, how strongly do they form attachements, how well do they learn new langauges, memeorzation of details, adaptability and how hardy they are in tought pressure situations. 

Most wash out here, becoming regular soldiers, clerks, or servants. They never know they were tested for something else.


Those who pass are told they've been selected and are taken from their training, into special agencies, and most have their families told their child died in an accident. A small funeral is held. The child's old name is buried.

They're moved to a secret training facility in isolated regions or keeps, Here they learn: that the empire is all that matters, its their family and the other recruits are their family. 

The tests and training however are extremely difficult and require reslience, forced to speak only target languages (Lorden, Aldergard, Mordantis, etc.) for months at a time, Isolation in cells for weeks, then suddenly surrounded by affection and community, Taught to distrust everyone, even fellow trainees and those who make mistakes are forced to be beaten by their fellow recruits, instructors force recruits in secret to make mock friendships which they are then forced to betray those mock friendships in training exercises.

They are also taught combat training yet not as much as the average soldiers as their time is highly occupied by other intellectual studies, it is mostly consistent of high intesnity and endurace training. as most trainees are taught to run over fighting, which is why they are also taught climbing vaulting and acrobatics.  

These Intellectual trainings also include encryption, dead drops, coded messages, target kingdom's history, memorization techniques and more.  these vary between agency to agency. which compete to have the best Insitus and Mortis.

Graduates have their housing, food and healing is all taken care of while in the capital Valcris, however, not when in duty.  

Many Ravanians revere these agents as legends from the shadows, and many do these jobs either due to this sense of honor, loyalty to Ravania or some are too psychologically damaged to want normal life while others believe completing their mission will finally earn them a place to belong, a puirpose or perhaps even redemption.

Insitus, are Silentum and Mortis are different paths one can take within these agencies each focuses on different aspects, while they each have their own higher ranking within them.

# Intelligence Command

**Magister Officiorum** - Master of Offices. Supreme spymaster, controls all imperial intelligence networks. Reports directly to the Kaiser. Always Equestrian or Senatore rank.

**Praefectus Insitorum** - Manages all long-term embedded agents across kingdoms, as well as Mortis, Silentiums and Delators.

**Magister Insitorum** - Master of the Planted. Runs entire networks of Insiti across a kingdom or region. Based in Ravania or secure location, never exposed. Manages dead drops, coordinates intelligence gathering, decides which Insitus to activate and when. Reports directly to Praefectus Insitorum.

**Delator** - Usually a rank mixed with that of the church, they are informants/Denouncers. Professional informers who report sedition, heresy, and disloyalty. Hated but necessary. Often masked to protect their identities.

Venator - Heretic Hunter. Specifically tracks and eliminates wild casters, followers of other religions. Religious zealots as much as spies. works closely with Delators.

##### Agent Ranks

The Mortis, Insitus and Silentium paths are equal in rank and their advancement within the respective Intelligence command as well.

**Primus Mortis** - Senior operative with multiple successful high-value eliminations. Trusted for the most dangerous hits. Can lead small teams of Mortis. Often enhanced with Sigilist protections or Aurian blessings for combat advantage. this rank usually requires the ability to cast Greschian Sorceries. 

**Mortis** - Elite spy-assassins who handle high-value eliminations and dangerous reconnaissance. Often drawn from Praetorians or Gresch sorcerers using scrying.

**Carnifex Silentium** - Made dozens of kills without ever being suspected. Trusted for the most sensitive eliminations - foreign royalty, allied nobles who've become liabilities, internal traitors at highest levels. Can arrange deaths months in advance with elaborate setups. 

**Silentium** - Assassin specialists who ensure targets die quietly without political complications or riots.

**Insitus Primus** - Senior deep cover agent who has successfully completed at least one major mission. Trusted to handle multiple assets or recruit new local agents. May coordinate several junior Insitus in the same region without direct contact. in the last few years this rank many times requires at least an ability to cast Sigilisim to at least its most basic form.
