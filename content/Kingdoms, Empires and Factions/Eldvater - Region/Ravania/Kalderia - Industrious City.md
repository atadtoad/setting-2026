## KALDERIA

### Overview

Kalderia is a major trade and industrial city within the Ravanian Empire. Its multitude of industries and facilities make it extremely valuable to Imperial interests. The city sits in a river valley that leads to a coastal basin, surrounded by hills that trap moisture and create its distinctive character.

### History

Historically, Kalderia was home to lamia-born monsters, including many Demiliss. When the Ravanian Empire crushed the entirety of the Eldvatr region, it slaughtered most of these lamias and built what Kalderia is today atop their ruins.

Much of the city's older infrastructure still bears the mark of lamia architecture and culture. Ancient aqueducts and pools of water remain functional, repurposed by Ravanian industry. Small holes in certain walls lead to underground passages designed to be traversed only by lamia bodies, twisting and narrow in ways human forms cannot easily follow.

To this day, rumors persist of stray lamias living below the city in hidden underground ruins, survivors of a once-proud civilization lurking in the flooded darkness.

### Industries

Kalderia's industrial heart consists of chemical works, foundries, and smelting operations, primarily serving the Empire's manufacturing needs.

The tanning and dyeing industries use copper-based mordants such as verdigris (copper acetate) and green dyes, boiling vast vats that release copper particles into the air. Brass and bronze foundries work with copper alloys, releasing more copper particles when casting and working the metal. Glass manufactories use copper oxide to produce green glass, releasing copper-tinted dust and fumes throughout the process.

### Geography and Climate

Kalderia is a verdant city with high humidity. The river valley geography traps cool air and moisture, while the surrounding hills prevent fog from dissipating. The valley itself remains cooler than the surrounding regions, intensifying the fog that settles within. This effect diminishes toward the city's outskirts.

The natural geography creates frequent fog from the water and trapped air. The copper industry adds a distinctive green tinge to the mist, strongest in early morning and evening when temperature differences peak. The city perpetually wears a veil of greenish fog that clings to streets and leaves a fine patina on older buildings.

### The Underground

The lower sewers of Kalderia contain gated areas that are locked and warded to prevent citizens from accidentally entering old lamian ruins. What lies beyond those gates remains a matter of rumor and official silence.

### Law Enforcement

Kalderia employs a few Noxificer enforcers, an uncommon and somewhat scandalous practice. However, their immunity to most poisons proves invaluable when dealing with offenders who are themselves Noxificers or wild mages. Kalderia hosts more Noxificers than any other city in the Ravanian Empire, both among criminals and those who hunt them.

### Population

The population of Kalderia is predominantly Veist human. A few Demiliss hide in the slums, surviving through smuggling and staying invisible. Rare Gigamar can be found doing menial labor in the industrial districts.
