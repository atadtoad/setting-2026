## The Astralegis

The Astralegis is headquartered in Thangard, with the Arch-Magister advising the King on magical matters. Membership requires demonstrated magical ability and an oath of loyalty to the crown. Internal ranks are based on skill and contribution rather than birth, allowing talented commoners to rise alongside nobles.

The organization advises the crown on magical matters and threats, trains new Arcanists in controlled and sanctioned magic, studies ancient ruins and magical phenomena across Lorden, and provides magical support to the military when needed. They compete with the Myrk Order for royal favor and resources, a rivalry that occasionally turns bitter.

The Astralegis officially opposes unregulated magic use and distinguishes sharply between their own "tamed" Arcanist magic and the "wild" hedge magic practiced by Wisewomen and Cunningfolk. Tensions exist between the two traditions over questions of legitimacy and safety. The Astralegis hunted witches in the past during periods of paranoia, though current policy favors observation rather than persecution. Occasional incidents still occur.
