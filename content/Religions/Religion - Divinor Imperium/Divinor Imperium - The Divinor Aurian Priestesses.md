# The Divinor Aurian Priestesses
These women of high zeal and fervor do fulfil many roles in the empire, from hunting heretics,  writing holy books, copying Grimoires, dealing with holidays, logistics and even bearing the childern of the Emperor.

They are known to be extremely vicious and inquisitive hunting any whif of heresy and descent or critic towards the emperor, while one may critize him publicly, doing it near a Priestess is paramount to suicide. 

## Recruitment Method
The priestesses of the Divinor Imperium are exclusively women, taken at a young age and trained in the Divinor Imperium Grimoire. This grimoire contains Aurian sorcery, a special branch of Greschian magic invented by the Pontifex Maxima of old. Only specific individuals can cast this augmented sorcery. If a child cannot wield it by age nine, she does not continue on the path to priesthood. She is either released back to her family or recruited as a Scriba Sacra to copy sacred texts.

## Aurian Sorcerery
A special branch of Greschian magic invented by the Pontifex Maxima of old. Only specific individuals can wield this augmented sorcery. Young girls are tested by age nine. Those who cannot manifest the gift do not continue on the path to priesthood. They are either released back to their families or recruited as Scriba Sacra to copy sacred texts.

Casting Aurian sorcery requires some innate potency for Greschian sorceries. However, it does not require one to bind themselves to the Circle of Greschian sorcery, nor are Aurian practitioners connected to the realm of Gheliach.

The innate potency to cast Aurian sorcery usually stems from Greschian bloodline. This bloodline grants potential and connection, yet the power itself flows from zeal and fervor of faith toward the Kaiser. Intense devotion manifests as magical force.

Aurian practitioners carry innate connection to the Gheliach realm through their bloodline heritage. However, they do not bind themselves to Gheliach as Greschian sorcerers do. Instead, they draw upon this connection indirectly through a complex ritual first developed by Kaiser Margric I.

The first emperor discovered that gold amplifies Greschian magical flow. He developed a ritual that binds gold directly into the anatomy of priestesses. This internal gold acts as a conduit, allowing them to channel power from their Gheliach bloodline connection without requiring the dangerous soul-binding that Greschian sorcerers undergo. Faith and zeal fuel the magic, while the gold-infused body channels it safely.

This gold integration grants a peculiar side effect: Aurian priestesses can sense gold and distinguish it from other metals through scent or taste alone. A mere whiff or bite reveals the metal's nature.

Once a woman casts Aurian sorcery, she becomes unable to practice any other form of magic. The ritual permanently alters her anatomy in ways that prevent bonding to other magical traditions.

Women who wield Aurian sorcery gain remarkable longevity, easily living to ages of 170 or beyond if not killed by other means. They remain visually unchanged from ordinary humans, yet they never lose their youth. They simply die peacefully in their sleep when their time comes.

Unlike Greschian sorcery, Aurian magic excels at strengthening allies, inflaming emotions of zeal and devotion, and weaving powerful magical bindings and shields. The strength of these sorceries depends entirely on the caster's fervor. The more loyalty, devotion, and passionate belief a priestess holds for the Emperor, the more powerful her magic becomes.

Male Priests only exists as Dilectus within a golden cage.

### Physical Changes

Aurian sorcery invigorates its practitioners far beyond what Greschian sorcerers experience. This vitality manifests as heightened senses. Their hearing sharpens. Colors appear more vivid, details become clearer, and scents grow more distinct. These women develop refined tastes, preferring only luxury wines and the finest foods. The heightened vitality also makes them intensely sexually voracious.

Yet these enhanced senses bring complications. Aurian priestesses grow hungrier but pickier about their food. They become acutely aware of bloating, nausea, and any physical debilitation. Diseases and plagues affect them far more severely than ordinary humans despite their extended lifespans. This sensitivity makes many priestesses obsessively wary of illness.

Aurian sorcery ends a woman's monthly cycles while leaving her fertile until the day she dies. This stems from the enhanced healing these priestesses possess, a trait they share with the Kaiser himself, though to a significantly lesser degree. Wounds close faster. Ailments that would linger in others pass quickly.

Like Greschian sorcerers, Aurian priestesses can control aspects of their blood and vitality. They heal themselves from minor afflictions with ease: hangovers vanish, concussions mend, drunkenness clears in moments. This control extends to fertility. The tenets of Divinor Imperium forbid priestesses from bearing children except with the Kaiser or through specific appointed roles within the church. Aurian magic allows them to enforce this restriction absolutely.
