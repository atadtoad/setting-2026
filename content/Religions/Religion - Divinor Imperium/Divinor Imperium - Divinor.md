# The Divinor Imperium
The Ravanian Empire follows the religion of Divinor Imperium, which venerates Kaiser Margric Artas Ordos III as divine. His divinity proves itself repeatedly. The Kaiser has never fallen in single combat and cannot die. Wounds heal within seconds. He suffers no sickness or debilitation. His body remains sculpted in warrior's prime, yet he wields the knowledge and power of the highest ranking Greschian sorcerer alongside masterful command of Sigilism.

The Divinor Imperium refuses to acknowledge the old gods of Veistyria, deeming them dead and forgotten. They forsook the capital of Valk in its darkest hour. Mentioning them, writing of them, or worshipping them constitutes heresy punishable by execution.

## The Tenets
Margric Artas Ordos is the one true god made flesh. His descendants carry his divine essence. To deny the Kaiser's divinity is heresy punishable by death.

Wealth belongs to the god-emperor. Citizens must offer gold coins to the priesthood as tribute. Silver, copper, and platinum are rejected as unworthy. Generosity to the church demonstrates devotion.

False religions poison the soul. Reiteglut, Aldergard's saint-worship, Lorden's wild faith - all are abominations. Believers in foreign gods must convert, flee, or die. Tolerance is treason.

Priestesses bear children only with the Kaiser or Dilecti. Unauthorized pregnancy violates divine law. Aurian magic enforces this absolutely. The god-emperor's bloodline is sacred above all others.

The Pontifex Maxima interprets divine will. Provincial priestesses enforce sacred law. To defy the church is to defy the Kaiser himself. Disobedience merits death.

Faith without fervor is hollow. True believers burn with passion for the Kaiser. Devotion must be absolute, unwavering, and violent when necessary. Doubt is weakness.

Every household maintains a bust of Kaiser Margric. Candles burn in his honor. Daily prayers are mandatory. Neglect invites investigation by Venatores.
