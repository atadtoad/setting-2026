She was born to a family that runs an inn on the side of Aldemont, just at the outskirts of Ostvallen, a village renowned for its milk and dairy. The inn sees few customers, perched as it is on a mountain ravine where a stream trickles down from the peaks and forms a small waterfall. A stone bridge connects their side to the neighboring mountain, and from the stream they harvest fresh water and run a mill to grind whatever wheat they buy into flour. Growing up on those slopes, she heard old stories about the deep caverns where the Lorins first emerged during the Age of Grays. Sometimes she found ancient markers and worn carvings half-buried in the mountain trails, remnants of that distant era.

Her mother is a Wisewoman, a witch with an always slightly off smile and an overly understanding attitude, watching her daughter like a raven watches like a shiny gold coin. Her father is a Landed Knight, granted the estate as reward from the crown for some heroic deed he never speaks of. He claims he was simply in the right place at the right time during his days as an Errant Knight. When offered lands for his service, he chose this small mountainside plot with its abandoned inn rather than a larger, more prosperous holding. Why he made that choice, she does not know. He only ever said he loved this place, and that was enough for him. Regardless of his title, he considers himself an innkeep through and through, and a damn excellent cook.

As a child, a dwarf friend of her father's gifted her a large book filled with illustrations of monsters, Magbeasts, mages, and heroes. Fairy tales depicting the Sagas of the Merry Three and other legends from ages past. The dwarf came from Veiliss, where his kind have traded with the Lorins since the days of Vallard the First. He visited occasionally, bringing news from deeper in the mountains and exotic ingredients her father could not find elsewhere. Her father taught her to read using recipe notes scrawled on parchment, sounding out ingredients and instructions until the letters made sense. Her mother taught her to read stories, sitting beside her and guiding her through the pages of the dwarf's book until she could follow the tales on her own. She wanted more. As she grew, she asked for books, and her parents managed to find some, though they were expensive. Inside she discovered stories of places, ruins, and other fascinating things.

She wishes to become a chronicler and a cook. Someone who explores the world, follows heroes into ruins, and writes their deeds into books. First, however, she needs scribing materials.

Like her father, she loves food. Exposure to small amounts of Magbeast meat from youth has left her able to consume most things without getting sick. She helped her father in the kitchen from a young age and developed a passion for the culinary. Many times she walked down to Ostvallen to fetch supplies for the inn, building a strong physique from the mountain trails. She dreams of writing down unique recipes and trying new ingredients and spices from across the world.

Yet alongside cooking, she holds a deep love for animals. [[Religions/Deities/Distinction in Deities \|Alderbeasts]] fascinate her most, particularly their tendency to lair in ancient ruins.

She has disadvantages in pursuing her dreams. Her eyesight is poor, requiring large round spectacles. Her mother claims her face has not fully grown yet and her eyes are still catching up.

She wishes to join the [[Kingdoms, Empires and Factions/Norvast  - Region/Lorden/Auxiliary Brigade - Vildrgard\|Vildrgard]]. To go on adventures chronicling heroes while cooking their meals for coin. To find ancient texts, uncover ruins, and learn mysteries. She knows magic exists, and her mother is called a witch, yet she has never seen anyone perform it. Only alchemy, used to help villagers with rashes and aches. Her parents claim the two are similar. The only magical thing she owns is a plain necklace with a small river stone. Her mother claimed to have enchanted it one night when she refused to sleep, a bribe to get her to bed. She wears it still, even knowing it is just a simple stone.

**Father**

Her father is now in his early thirties. He grew up in Ostvallen as the younger son of a lesser noble family. They owned little beyond an old inn, and even that slipped away as they neared financial ruin. As a boy, he became fascinated with an older girl living on the outskirts of the village, an outcast no one wanted to approach. She was a few years his elder, strange and compelling where others saw only someone to avoid.

When he came of age at fourteen, his family sent him out as a squire, eventually becoming an Errant Knight to make a name for himself and restore their fortunes. He never wanted to fight. He wanted to learn a trade, to run an inn as he had helped do in his youth. But he was good at fighting whether he liked it or not, and so he fought.

While on the road, word reached him that his mother had died of disease and his father had left for Thangard to start a new family there. He had nothing to return to. Then came a battle he never speaks of, some heroic deed that earned him elevation to Landed Knight. It was during this conflict that he met a dwarf from Veiliss, a friendship that would last the rest of his life. When offered his choice of lands as reward, he asked only for a small abandoned inn on the mountainside above Ostvallen, a place he had frequented during his travels. They granted it, perhaps puzzled by such a modest request.

He returned to Ostvallen at eighteen and sought out the strange girl from his childhood. She had become a Wisewoman living in a hut in the forest. He married her anyway. He insisted on a [[Religions/Religion - The Myrkvargr Covenant/The Myrkvargr Covenant \|Grey Shepherd]] performing the ceremony under the [[Religions/Religion - The Myrkvargr Covenant/The Myrkvargr Covenant \|Myrkvargr Covenant]] rather than an [[Religions/Religion - Alderdyr/Alderdyr\|Alderdyr]] rite. Not because he doubted the old faith, he keeps shrines to both, but because he wanted no question of legitimacy for any children they might have. His father was a harsh man who had abandoned him, and though it likely would never matter, he would not risk his offspring being called bastards by whatever family his father had built in Thangard.

He considers himself an innkeep through and through, and a damn excellent cook. The knighthood is born into not out of choice.

**Mother**

Her mother is in her mid-thirties now. She comes from a family of nomads who travel the inner ridges of Aldemont, trading as they go. During one such stop in Ostvallen when she was young, they abandoned her. She does not speak of why. Left alone in an unfamiliar village, she survived on scraps until a younger boy began sneaking food to her. It helped more than he knew.

Eventually the local Cunningman took her in and taught her his craft. Loneliness or pity, she never learned which. Rumors followed her regardless. Whispers of witchcraft brought trouble, and trouble brought more whispers. She left Ostvallen and traveled the land for a time. She joined the [[Kingdoms, Empires and Factions/Norvast  - Region/Lorden/Auxiliary Brigade - Vildrgard\|Vildrgard]] at some point, though she was kicked out for reasons she does not discuss.

Years later she returned to Ostvallen to visit the Cunningman who had raised her, only to learn he had died just days before her arrival. She stayed for a time after that, grieving in the hut he had left behind, uncertain where else to go. A month or so later, a handsome young knight appeared and swept her off her feet, offering her a life in some mountainside inn. Perhaps it was a moment of weakness, or a longing for real company after so many years alone. She stayed. They married in a hasty ritual performed by a [[Religions/Religion - The Myrkvargr Covenant/The Myrkvargr Covenant \|Grey Shepherd]], a faith she does not follow. She keeps to [[Religions/Religion - Alderdyr/Alderdyr\|Alderdyr]] and its [[Religions/Deities/Distinction in Deities \|Alderbeasts]], tending a small shrine the villagers do not see.

She had her daughter at twenty. She watches her now like a raven, smiling that slightly off smile, and speaks of magic as though it were merely alchemy. Villagers still climb the mountain path from time to time, quietly seeking remedies for rashes, aches, and women's troubles. They pay coin or trade goods, and it helps keep the inn afloat. She is seen as more proper now, a knight's wife rather than a forest witch, though the marriage has done the father's family name no favors among those who remember what she was.
