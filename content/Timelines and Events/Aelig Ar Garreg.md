She was born to a family that runs an inn on the side of Aldemont, just at the outskirts of Ostvallen, a village renowned for its milk and dairy. The inn sees few customers, perched as it is on a mountain ravine where a stream trickles down from the peaks and forms a small waterfall. A stone bridge connects their side to the neighboring mountain, and from the stream they harvest fresh water and run a mill to grind whatever wheat they buy into flour. Growing up on those slopes, she heard old stories about the deep caverns where the Lorins first emerged during the Age of Grays. Sometimes she found ancient markers and worn carvings half-buried in the mountain trails, remnants of that distant era.

Her mother is a Wisewoman, a witch with an always slightly off smile and an overly understanding attitude, watching her daughter like a raven watches a shiny coin. Her father is a Landed Knight, granted the estate as reward from the crown for some heroic deed he never speaks of. He claims he was simply in the right place at the right time while serving in a levy. Regardless of his title, he considers himself an innkeep through and through, and a damn excellent cook.

As a child, a dwarf friend of her father's gifted her a large book filled with illustrations of monsters, Magbeasts, mages, and heroes. Fairy tales depicting the Sagas of the Merry Three and other legends from ages past. The dwarf came from Veiliss, where his kind have traded with the Lorins since the days of Vallard the First. He visited occasionally, bringing news from deeper in the mountains and exotic ingredients her father could not find elsewhere. She wanted more. As she grew, she asked for books, and her parents managed to find some, though they were expensive. Inside she discovered stories of places, ruins, and other fascinating things.

She wishes to become a chronicler and a cook. Someone who explores the world, follows heroes into ruins, and writes their deeds into books. First, however, she needs scribing materials.

Like her father, she loves food. Exposure to small amounts of Magbeast meat from youth has left her able to consume most things without getting sick. She helped her father in the kitchen from a young age and developed a passion for the culinary. Many times she walked down to Ostvallen to fetch supplies for the inn, building a strong physique from the mountain trails. She dreams of writing down unique recipes and trying new ingredients and spices from across the world.

Yet alongside cooking, she holds a deep love for animals. Alderbeasts fascinate her most, particularly their tendency to lair in ancient ruins.

She has disadvantages in pursuing her dreams. Her eyesight is poor, requiring large round spectacles. Her mother claims her face has not fully grown yet and her eyes are still catching up.

She wishes to join the Vildrgard. To go on adventures chronicling heroes while cooking their meals for coin. To find ancient texts, uncover ruins, and learn mysteries. She knows magic exists, and her mother is called a witch, yet she has never seen anyone perform it. Only alchemy, used to help villagers with rashes and aches. Her parents claim the two are similar. The only magical thing she owns is a plain necklace with a small river stone. Her mother claimed to have enchanted it one night when she refused to sleep, a bribe to get her to bed. She wears it still, even knowing it is just a simple stone.
