## Greschian Sorcery

Greschian practitioners are exceedingly rare outside Ravania's ranks and often eccentric in nature. They rely heavily on grimoires, also known as spellbooks, to conduct their craft. These tomes serve dual purposes: comprehensive reference sheets for their spells and collections of pre-made circles and ritualistic tools prepared before any magical venture.

Greschian spellcasters may hone their expertise in various disciplines. Some master divination, others summonings or complex rituals. A few even specialize in offensive spellwork. A properly prepared Greschian magician can ready spheres of demon-fire to hurl at enemies, summon monsters to their aid, or scry upon their foes as any spy might.

The true strength of Greschian magic lies in summoning demonic powers. This capability renders Greschian magic forbidden in most lands beyond Ravania's borders. Even the ability to cast such magic requires a specific ritual known as Demonic Bonding.

While anyone may attempt to bind themselves to the Circle of Greschian sorcery, only those with Greschian blood can do so safely and wield potent power from the start.

In ancient Greschia, only those of royal blood were sanctioned to bind to the Bounded Circle. This was sacred law, meant to keep the power and burden of Gheliach within the ruling family.

In current times, the Ravanian Empire claims this magic as their rightful inheritance. Only those of Ravanian blood, the descendants of both Veistyria and Greschia, are permitted to learn Greschian sorceries and attempt the binding. The empire enforces this as both religious doctrine and imperial law.

However, nothing physically prevents someone from reaching the Ruined Kingdom of Greschia and attempting to bind to the circle themselves. The Bounded Circle does not ask for permission or check bloodlines before allowing an attempt. But any non-Ravanian who successfully binds to the circle is declared a heretical enemy of the empire. Ravania considers such individuals to be thieves of sacred power, and they will be hunted down and destroyed without mercy.

### Demonic Blood-Bonding
Greschian magic functions as Bounded Circle magic, governed by a physical object: an ancient stone carving beneath the throne room of Greschia. The old queen kept this artifact close at hand, using it as both a Bounded Circle and summoning circle combined. Legends recovered from Greschia's ruined libraries claim she could summon demons powerful enough to rival Colossi.

Unlike some Bounded Circles, Greschian magic demands sacrifice. A practitioner must spill their own blood and undergo a ritual binding to the realm from which Greschian power flows. According to grimoires, this is the realm of Gheliach, a domain of raw, unshaped demonic energy.

Blood sacrificed upon the circle binds the practitioner to it, allowing them to channel power from Gheliach. Yet rumors persist that the ritual binds not just the body but the soul itself to Gheliach, sacrificing one's future self in exchange for present power. Only those who can reach Greschia and remain untroubled by such costs may become Greschian casters.
