Vendants are rarely viewed as true mages or spellcasters. The stigma of being "wild mages" follows them regardless, a stain that marks them particularly in regions like Ravania. Yet their existence and acceptance was sanctioned by Kaiser Margric himself, granting them a place within the Empire. Whether this was purely pragmatic utility or influenced by rumors that one of his sons was himself a Vendant remains a matter of quiet speculation.

Their senses are dialed to inhuman sharpness. They see the world in vivid clarity, track scents like bloodhounds, possess eyesight rivaling eagles, move with the instincts and agility of hunting cats, and strike with the strength of bulls. Despite these enhancements, their bodies remain fundamentally human. Overexertion takes its toll. They break and bleed like anyone else, just harder to catch and harder to kill.

Vendants are incapable of using magic in any form. This makes them exceptional mage-hunters, though the work earns them little respect. They are called Hounds or Dogs in derogatory terms, treated as hunting animals pointed at targets rather than people with agency in many circles. The comparison is not entirely unfair. Rumors claim they originated during the Witchfires, created through alchemical intervention and infusion techniques designed specifically to hunt and execute magic users. Stories persist that they are drawn to magic itself, able to smell it in the air the way predators smell blood.

They possess an ability similar to certain magbeasts and monsters, a natural capacity to deflect some measure of magical force with their bodies. But this protection comes at a cost. Their inability to cast magic extends to receiving it as well. They cannot be healed by the touch of an Aurian priestess or strengthened by battle blessings. Healing magic slides off them like water off stone.

This forces reliance on alchemy. Their bodies regenerate slightly faster than ordinary humans, but only marginally. What sets them apart is their superior ability to metabolize poisons and toxins. This allows them to use more extreme alchemical remedies that would kill normal men, accelerating their recovery through controlled poisoning and aggressive chemical intervention. They heal through violence done to their own bodies, because gentler methods simply do not work.
